# Ashes of Eden — first alpha

They hanged Elian and buried him outside the fence. He got up anyway.

The first public alpha: **Chapter I is playable start to finish**, in the browser, on desktop and
on Android. Free and open source. Things will break — please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Saaayurii/ashes-of-eden/issues) with what happened and the
version from the main menu's corner.

**▶ Play in the browser:** https://saaayurii.github.io/ashes-of-eden/ (full screen on Play; on an
iPhone, Share → Add to Home Screen)

## Downloads

| File | Platform | Note |
|---|---|---|
| `ashes-of-eden-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11 | unsigned: SmartScreen → More info → Run anyway |
| `ashes-of-eden-macos.zip` | macOS | unsigned: right-click the app → Open |
| `ashes-of-eden-linux.zip` | Linux x86_64 | `chmod +x` and run |
| `*.apk` | Android | debug-signed, install from the file; arrives a little after the rest |
| `ashes-of-eden-web.zip` | any browser | the same build as the Pages site, to host yourself |

## What is in it

- **Chapter I** — 15 painted rooms across 8 places, from the village to the gate of Hell, with two
  forks in the road; cutscenes, captions and spoken lines.
- **Combat** — a three-hit chain, roll, block and parry/riposte, backstabs on the unaware, ground
  slam, wall jump, and special moves (lunge, cleave, sweep). Every enemy blow has a readable
  wind-up.
- **17 kinds of enemy**, a mid-boss and a boss; elites with affixes (Ironclad, Swift, Brutal,
  Enduring) that leave a cache behind.
- **47 gifts on three paths** — grace, temptation, will — with rarities, active skills and 16
  resonances. Which way you lean shows on Elian's body, in the music and in what the world says to
  you; there is no "true good ending".
- **Between nights** — the reliquary (Ash buys relics), five vials of wrath, omens, the night of
  the day with streaks, 32 deeds, a bestiary with a codex and a chronicle of your nights.
- **Trades** — blood altars, cursed chests, refusing a hand of gifts.
- **Two players** — online co-op and a 1v1 duel, crossplay including the browser.
- **Accessibility** — text size, reduced flashes, game speed, auto-attack, block toggle, rebinding,
  gamepad and touch controls.
- **Languages** — English, Русский, Українська, 中文.

## Known rough edges

- Builds are unsigned (Windows, macOS) or debug-signed (Android); see docs/RELEASE.md.
- The Web build downloads about 100 MB on the first visit and carries a lighter music library.
- Rooms are separate screens joined by a short transition; a seamless world is being explored.
