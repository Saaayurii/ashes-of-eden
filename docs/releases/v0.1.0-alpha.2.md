# Ashes of Eden — second alpha

Chapter I remains playable from beginning to end. This update focuses on the journey between its places, combat readability, story presentation, and online play.

## What changed

- Five playable, hand-painted passages now connect the graveyard, swamp, catacombs, crypt, ashes and Abyss gate. Their chapter cards have matching artwork, including a new crypt-to-gate card.
- Room backdrops have depth confined to architectural openings and subtle motion that responds to combat and the hero. Passage floors and arrival points are checked against the actual player body and visible boots.
- Cutscenes have cinematic framing, boss title cards, voiced Knight and Preacher moments, clearer chapter-ending beats, and music that steps back under dialogue.
- Online co-op can rejoin after a connection drop or join later. LAN discovery and relay room codes make finding a game easier.
- The practice yard teaches guard, volley and stalking. Keyboard and mouse controls can be rebound.
- Backdrop effects have been optimized, including a low-motion option for players and slower hardware.
