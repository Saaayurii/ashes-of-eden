# Ashes of Eden — notes for AI assistants

Godot 4.7 / GDScript, 2D pixel action roguelite. Open source, community-driven, 4 locales (en, ru, uk, zh_CN).

## Conventions
- Content is data: `data/**/*.json` (abilities, enemies, dialogues), strings in `localization/strings.csv`. Never hardcode player-facing text; use a key and add all 4 columns.
- Autoloads: `EventBus` (signals), `Data` (JSON loader), `Game` (run state: hidden alignment grace/temptation/will, flags, gifts), `Settings` (locale).
- New ability effect type → `scripts/combat/ability_system.gd` **and** `scripts/tools/validate_data.gd`. A new gift *mechanic* is usually a new key in `Player.BASE_STATS` (default 0 = no change) plus the code that reads it: also add it to `STATS` in the validator, a cap in `AbilitySystem.CAPS`, and a check in `gift_test.gd`. Every gift has a `rarity`. Same for enemy behaviours / attack types (`scripts/enemies/enemy.gd`).
- Rooms are generated once by `tools/rooms/generate_rooms.py`; a room tuned by hand in the editor must be removed from that table.
- Hero frames: `assets/sprites/elian_frames.tres` + `slash_frames.tres` (128×64 cells, feet at x=68 on the bottom row, body 43 px tall — a hair under the village knight; effects 80×48). Regenerate with `tools/art/slice_hero.py --contact` (one entry per generated sheet in `SHEETS`; check `tools/art/contact/hero.png`) then `tools/art/build_elian_frames.py`; don't hand-edit. The player's `Body` sits at (0,−20): the cell's bottom row is 4 px above the collision's bottom, the same lift the enemies have, so his boots clear the foreground grass like theirs.
- Hero animations the code relies on (`scripts/player/player.gd`): `rest`/`draw`/`idle` (sword sheathed until an enemy is aware or we swing), `attack`/`attack2`/`attack3` (the chain), `thrust` (backstab pose), `rising` (airborne swing), `dash_strike` (swing out of a roll), `jump`/`fall`/`climb`/`land`, `hurt`/`knockback`, `roll`, `death`, `wake`, `talk`, `guard`. The swings have their white arc drawn in; the `Slash` sprite only adds the path colour (`gold`/`red`) and the finisher's `heavy` arc.
- Every enemy attack has a wind-up the player can read; never add an unreadable instant hit.
- Enemies start unaware (`PATROL`): they pace around their spawn and only hunt once they see a player (cone in front + raycast, `sight`/`patrol` in the JSON), get touched, hit, or a neighbour shouts. A hit on an unaware enemy is a backstab (`Player.stats.backstab_multiplier`, decided by the enemy/host in `_apply_damage`). Bosses and boss summons start aware.
- Settings live in the `Settings` autoload (locale, bus volumes, fullscreen, shake, key overrides). New audio goes to the `Music` or `SFX` bus, never Master.
- Every enemy sounds different when you hit it: its `material` in the JSON picks the impact clips (`<id>_impact_1..5`, fallback `hit_<material>`, generic `hit` for props). Whoever swung plays it (`Enemy.impact_sound()` asked from `Player._attack`), so it lands with no round trip online; the creature's own cry (`<id>_hurt`) is separate. Every cue comes in three takes (two for the death rattle) — one throat, re-rolled — so a crowd of one kind is still a crowd. Regenerate with `tools/audio/generate_voices.py`; it is deterministic, so a regeneration is not a diff.
- Music is a mood, not a file: `Audio.music("graveyard")` picks from `data/music/playlists.json` (157 tracks, 11 moods, never the same twice in a row). New track → a row in `tools/audio/music_manifest.json` (free/attribution-compatible source, its playlists) then `python3 tools/audio/fetch_music.py`, which downloads, trims to 150 s, normalises to −18 LUFS and rewrites the playlists and `assets/CREDITS.md`. Verify a candidate URL before adding it: a 404 is the usual failure. After changing the library run `python3 tools/export/music_filters.py`: Android and Web presets carry only a light subset, and `Audio._pick` skips tracks a build does not have.
- Sound is a name, not a file: `Audio.play(&"hit")`, `Audio.play_at(&"enemy_death", pos)`, `Audio.music("graveyard")`. A row in `MIX` (level, pitch, spread) in `scripts/autoload/audio.gd` balances it, a row in `assets/CREDITS.md` credits it. Third-party audio is CC0 only; loudness and pitch are set in code, never baked into the file.
- **Takes, not clips.** A name ending in `_<number>` is a take of the name before it (`Audio._base_name`), and asking for the bare name plays one of them at random — that is the whole cure for a fight that sounds like a loop. `swing`, `step`, `block`, `parry`, `enemy_swing`, `draw` and every creature cue have several. Extra takes from CC0 packs → a row in `tools/audio/sfx_manifest.json` then `python3 tools/audio/fetch_sfx.py` (downloads the pack once, peak-normalises the members, writes `<name>_10…` and the credits). Placeholders with no real clip yet come from `tools/audio/generate_sfx.py`.
- **Story lines are spoken, and nothing depends on it.** `Audio.speak("DLG_…")` looks for `assets/audio/voice/<locale>/<KEY>.mp3` and returns its length, or 0.0 when nobody recorded it; `dialogue_box.gd` holds the caption for whichever is longer. A missing file is not a bug — an unvoiced line is a caption, which is how the game played before any of this. Who sounds like whom is `tools/audio/voice_cast.json`, rendered by `python3 tools/audio/generate_speech.py` (Piper, local, offline). **A rendered line inherits the licence of the voice's dataset**, and `LICENSE-ASSETS.md` bans non-commercial, so several of Piper's best voices (`en_US-ryan`, `ru_RU-ruslan`, `zh_CN-xiao_ya`, `en_US-lessac`) are refused by name in the script. Check a new voice's `MODEL_CARD` on `rhasspy/piper-voices` — the `.onnx.json` says nothing about licensing. Turned off by `Settings.speech` (Settings → Spoken lines), which `Audio.speak` checks first. Pitch shifting goes through the WORLD vocoder so formants stay put — a resampler drags them along and a voice four semitones down stops sounding like a person; `"formants": false` in the cast opts out, and only the two parts that are not people use it. Full story: `docs/VOICE.md`.
- **Ground slam and wall grab are free moves, not gifts.** Down in the air (`_start_slam`) drops him at `SLAM_SPEED` and hits everything beside where he lands for `SLAM_DAMAGE_SHARE` of a swing; it needs `SLAM_MIN_HEIGHT` of clear world below (`_room_to_slam`, world layer only — a jump-through ledge is not a floor to slam onto), and on the ground `down` is still the drop-through. Holding the stick into a wall in mid-air (`_update_wall_grab`) caps the fall at `WALL_SLIDE_SPEED` and lets `jump` push off away from it, handing back the air jump rather than adding one. Both are locked out during a roll, a mantle, a heal and a scripted pose, and both are cleared on death and revive. The touch stick already presses `move_down`, so neither needed a new button. `traversal_moves_test.gd` covers them.
- **A gift can arrive on a later night.** `"unlock_nights": N` in `data/abilities` keeps it out of `Run._roll_gifts` until `Profile.data.nights >= N` (`Run.gift_locked`), and on the night it arrives `Run._announce_unlocks` says so once over the opening. Ignored in a duel — both players must see the same cards. A gift without the field is unaffected, which is all of them but six.
- **The run's lean is audible, not only visible.** `Player._update_aura` already knows the dominant path and how far ahead it is; it hands both to `Audio.alignment_layer(path, strength)`, which crossfades a per-path loop (`assets/audio/music/layer_<path>.wav`, made by `tools/audio/generate_alignment_layers.py`) under whatever the room is playing. Silent below a lead of 2, and it fades out slower than it fades in so a brief swing does not make it flicker. The layers are not in any playlist, so `tools/export/music_filters.py` keeps them by name — otherwise light Android/Web builds would drop them. `alignment_test.gd` covers it (and does not trust `playing`: headless uses the dummy audio driver).
- **The lean also shows on the body.** `Player._mark_body(path, strength)` binds `assets/shaders/alignment_marks.gdshader` to the sprite: edge light for grace, veins for temptation, ash and desaturation for will. It reads the sprite's own alpha (four taps for the silhouette edge) rather than a painted mask, so it holds through every animation and costs no art. Below a lead of 2 the material stays bound with `strength` 0 — a no-op, cheaper than swapping materials each frame. Covered by `alignment_test.gd` alongside the music layer.
- **The Chinese has its own face in the build.** Neither Latin font has a CJK glyph; a desktop hides that behind a system fallback, a Web export cannot. `assets/fonts/NotoSerifSC-Subset.ttf` — Noto Serif SC, instanced to weight 400 and cut down to the characters `strings.csv` actually uses by `tools/art/make_cjk_font.py` (25 MB → 343 KB) — sits in the theme's fallback chain ahead of the system font. Serif, not sans: the game is set in EB Garamond and Forum, and Song is what belongs beside them. Add Chinese text with a character outside the subset and `validate_data.gd` fails; rerun the tool and commit the result.
- **Story flags are checked in both directions.** `validate_data.gd` collects every flag written (`set_flags` in a dialogue choice or a prop) and every flag read (a router's `branches`, a cutscene step's `if`/`unless`, plus `FLAGS_READ_IN_CODE` for the ones GDScript names). A flag read but never written is an error — that branch cannot be reached. A flag written but never read is a warning — a choice the world forgot to react to. Both fail silently in play, which is why they are caught here.
- Unique names (`%Name`) only resolve inside their own scene: from `run.gd` use `run_end.get_node("%RunEndStats")`, not `%RunEndStats`.
- Physics layers: 1 world (solid), 2 player, 3 enemies, 4 player hitbox, 5 ledges (one-way; walkers and the player collide with it, flyers don't, down+jump drops through). Side-view platformer (gravity in player/enemy scripts, not ProjectSettings).
- Level flow: `scenes/run/run.tscn` chains `scenes/rooms/*.tscn` (`ROOMS` in run.gd). Rooms are hand-made; enemies are `EnemySpawn` markers. Rooms have `width` and `height` (most are two screens tall, boss arenas one); the run sets the camera limits from them.
- Parallax2D: a layer sits at `camera × (1 − scroll_scale)`, so 1 = static in the world, 0 = glued to the screen. The 640×360 backdrops use ≈0.1 (see the comment in `generate_rooms.py`).
- The HUD minimap (`scripts/ui/minimap.gd`) draws the current room from `Room.map_rects()` plus the groups `player`, `enemies` (replicated `aware`), `npc`; nothing else is replicated for it.
- Menu/rooms use layers, not GIFs: painting + `assets/shaders/fog.gdshader` + CPUParticles2D + additive glow sprites with `flicker.gd`. Keep it CPUParticles (GL Compatibility + Web).
- Active skills are gifts with an effect `{"type": "skill", "skill": {kind, cooldown, damage, …}}`; kinds `nova` / `bolt` / `drain` live in `Player._cast_skill`, the key is `skill` (U / LB). One skill at a time; `Player.skill` is saved with the body.
- Lava and other burning ground: `hazards=[(x, y, w, h)]` in a room table → `scripts/rooms/hazard.gd`, which calls `Player.burn` (hurts through a roll, puts the body back on its last safe floor) and burns walkers.
- The hero shows the path he leans to (`Player._update_aura`): motes and a light tint once one counter leads the others by 2. Never show the numbers.
- Lighting: every light is a `GlowLight` (`scripts/fx/glow_light.gd`, made through `Fx.light` / `Fx.flash`), rooms get an `Ambient` CanvasModulate from the generator. Both obey `Settings.lighting`; never add a bare `PointLight2D`. One-shot effects go through `Fx` (`dust`, `ash`, `sparkle`, `debris`, `flash`, `trail`, `shadow`), not ad-hoc emitters.
- Draw order in a room: terrain and `DecorMid` behind characters, `DecorFront` (z 1) only for low clutter over the feet, weather z 2, Fx 4-6, numbers 10. Anything a body can be "behind" belongs in `DecorMid`, never in `DecorFront`.
- The first location is 13 painted panels (`tools/rooms/painted_rooms.py`, `assets/levels/`): the picture is the level, colliders trace its ledges. New panel → `tools/rooms/trace_panel.py <stamp> <name>`, pick the real ledges from the numbered overlay, keep entrance left / exit right, and let `check_reach` (runs on generation) prove the door and every walker are reachable.
- Platform art comes from `assets/decor/platforms/` (sliced by `tools/art/slice_batch9.py`, sizes in `manifest.json`); the generator lays pieces over the collider rects. Colliders stay flat rects in `Geometry`/`Ledges` (the minimap reads them).
- Saves (`Saves` autoload, `scenes/ui/save_menu.tscn`): autosave + 3 slots in `user://saves/`, solo only. A save is the Run's `checkpoint` — the state on entering the current room — so loading restarts that room; never snapshot mid-room. Rooms are saved by scene path, gifts by id; `Saves.validate` must keep accepting old saves (drop what no longer exists, refuse only what cannot be played). Anything new that a run carries between rooms goes into `Saves.capture` **and** `Saves.restore`. Tool scripts (`-s`) save into `user://saves_tools/`.
- UI that opens while the game is paused needs `process_mode = 3` (Always).
- Touch: `scripts/ui/touch_pad.gd` (the `TouchControls` node in run and duel) owns every touch — floating stick + one button per action, sized from the viewport's short side × `Settings.touch_scale`, safe-area aware, steps aside while the tree is paused. A new player action needs a button in its `LAYOUT`. It swallows the emulated left click touches produce (that click is `attack`).
- Playtests: the `Playtest` autoload writes every solo run to `user://playtest/*.jsonl` (off under `-s`); `scripts/tools/balance_probe.gd` measures duels and reads those logs into `docs/BALANCE_PROBE.md`. Tune `data/enemies` against that report, not by feel.
- Builds: `make build` (desktop + Web), `make android-image` then `make android` (debug-signed test APK). Light builds (Android, Web) get their exclude lists from `tools/export/music_filters.py`; the painted panels import as lossy WebP on purpose (their `.import` files are tracked).
- Full-screen feedback for the player's own eyes — the red vignette on a hit (`assets/shaders/vignette.gdshader`) and the eyelids blinking open when the body comes to — is `scripts/ui/screen_fx.gd`, the first child of a scene's `UI` layer (run and duel). It listens to `EventBus.player_hurt` / `player_waking`, which only our own body emits; the lids slot themselves in right after the HUD so bars vanish with the eyes shut but dialogue and menus stay on top.
- Secrets: a `destructible` prop with `reveals` (a bricked-up wall, `tools/art/make_secret_walls.py`) leaves a cache chest; a chest with `note` files a record (`data/notes`, bestiary "Records", read as a caption) and pays its `ash` only the first time. `secret_test.gd` covers every wall placed in a room.
- Tabs in GDScript. Files `snake_case`, nodes `PascalCase`, `%UniqueName` for nodes scripts reach into.

## Multiplayer (see docs/MULTIPLAYER.md)
- Two players, online, crossplay. Transport is WebSocket (`Net` autoload) because it is the only Godot peer that works in a Web export; do not reach for ENet.
- The host simulates rooms, enemies and score; every player body is simulated by its owner. A body is only ever hurt by its owner — `take_damage` forwards the hit instead of applying it when it is not.
- Never `get_tree().paused` — call `Net.set_paused`. Pausing freezes the synchronizers and desyncs the session.
- There may be two players in the tree: no `get_first_node_in_group("player")`; pick by `is_multiplayer_authority()` or act on all of them.
- Replicated properties are declared in code (`Net.attach_sync`, called from the spawner's spawn function before the node enters the tree), not in a `.tscn`.
- New enemy theatre (a telegraph, a swing, a beam) → an RPC next to `_net_strike`, guarded by `if Net.active`.

- Tool scripts under `scripts/tools/` are the only place allowed to be untyped w.r.t. game classes.

  `/Applications/Godot.app/Contents/MacOS/Godot --headless --path . -s scripts/tools/skill_test.gd` (the three active skills fire, hurt, heal, cool down)

## Design guardrails (see docs/GDD.md)
- Angels ≠ good, demons ≠ evil, God never appears on screen. No "true good ending".
- Core loop stays dumb (move / attack / dash / block / pick a gift). Depth goes into data and story.
- Every dialogue must be skippable. Three looks, one `DialogueBox`: the bottom panel (story choices), captions (`"blocking": false`, over play) and speech bubbles over the speaker's head for talking to NPCs on E (`play_bubble`; Elian's lines and the answers float over him, 1–3 / E / Esc). Only the panel may take `ui_accept` — it used to swallow the Space a cutscene skips on.
- Chapter I, scene by scene, flag by flag: `docs/CHAPTER1.md`. Keep it in step with `ROOMS`, `data/chapters/` and the scenes.
- The curtain is an autoload (`Curtain`, `scripts/autoload/curtain.gd`) so it outlives the scene under it: it burns a room away, hides the swap, and draws the chapter card when the place changes. Every seam goes through it — `Curtain.change_scene(path, keep_closed, while_black)` for menu/run/duel, `cover()`/`reveal()` around a room swap or the verdict. Places are data: `data/chapters/*.json` lists the rooms of each. Online the host sends `_net_cover`, waits for its own black and only then calls the swap (`Run._go_to_room`) — a peer that is still burning would free the enemies the host just spawned. `Settings.transitions` (full / short / off) scales every beat; a tool script (`godot -s`) gets `Curtain.instant = true` for free, which keeps `_load_room` synchronous.
- Cutscenes are data too: `data/cutscenes/*.json` played by `scripts/ui/cutscene.gd` (`$UI/Cutscene` in the run), attached to rooms by `intro_cutscene` / `outro_cutscene` in the generator tables. A scene `hold`s and must `release`; any button skips and the leftover steps apply instantly. `Game.cutscene` is how enemies know to freeze. New step type → `_run` in cutscene.gd **and** `CUTSCENE_STEPS` in the validator. Any step can carry `"if"` / `"unless"` (flags) or `"path"` — that is how one scene plays each choice differently. A choice inside a scene goes through `Run._cutscene_story` (online, the host asks once for both players). A person who exists only for a scene is an NPC with `"scripted": true` (hidden until `appear`, never talked to on E).
